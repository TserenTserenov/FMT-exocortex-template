## What are zeroth principles

Zeroth principles are the most general trans-disciplinary constraints. They eliminate incorrect models and make reasoning verifiable. They apply **in any domain**: engineering, science, business, and creative work.

Zeroth principles **discipline** all other levels: first, second, and third. Those who have mastered them do not confuse correlation with causation, do not build models that collapse under verification, and do not make decisions based on intuition.

## How to use

1. **New to the ecosystem** — start with this file, then read [hierarchy.md](hierarchy.md) to understand the level map.
2. **Verifying a decision** — check each of the 6 principles: does this decision violate any of them? Each file in [principles/](principles/) lists typical violations.
3. **Debating an approach** — return to the zeroth principles: which of ZP.1–ZP.6 is being violated?
